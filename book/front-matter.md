# Reminders GTD

An opinionated Getting Things Done setup for the iPhone and iPad **Reminders** app on **iOS 27** and **iPadOS 27**.

David Allen’s method is the foundation. This guide is independent work: it is not an Apple product and it is not endorsed by David Allen or the David Allen Company.

The canonical web edition is at [markheydon.me.uk/ios-reminders-gtd](https://markheydon.me.uk/ios-reminders-gtd/).

## About this edition

Chapters follow the same reading order as the web guide. The table of contents replaces the numbered list on the home page. Diagrams and setup tables are included; tap paths are written for iPhone and iPad.

Draft builds may be generated automatically for proofreading. Release editions are attached to [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/markheydon/ios-reminders-gtd/releases).
