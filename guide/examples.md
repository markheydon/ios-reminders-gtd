This is one ordinary week for you after a quiet couple of months. The point is the shape of the decisions, so you can copy the moves with your own words.

## Monday afternoon: the dump

You dump everything into **Inbox** on the walk home (Siri if typing is awkward):

- tax return
- tap still dripping
- call dentist
- maybe learn to bake bread
- Priya owes the damp photos
- parking fine on the kitchen table
- email accountant the questions
- idea: repaint the spare room

## Monday evening: clarify

| Inbox line | Decision | Where it ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tax return | More than one step, and you are committed | Project in Money: **Tax return filed**. Notes hold “pay anything owed” for later. Subtask: “Email accountant the questions” tagged `anywhere`. The separate “email accountant” inbox line is the same action, so it is deleted. |
| tap still dripping | A project, because someone has to look at it and then it has to be fixed | Project in Home: **Kitchen tap fixed**. Subtask: “Priya: photos of the damp patch” tagged `waiting`, When Messaging set to Priya. You realise the photos are about the tap, so “Priya owes the damp photos” is that same subtask. |
| call dentist | One phone call | **Next Actions**: “Call dentist and book a checkup” tagged `call`. |
| maybe learn to bake bread | Not a commitment | **Someday**, untagged. |
| parking fine on the kitchen table | One step, needs the house because the letter is there | **Next Actions**: “Pay the parking fine” tagged `home`. |
| idea: repaint the spare room | Not a commitment this month | **Someday**, untagged. |

**Inbox** is empty. **Anywhere** contains “Email accountant the questions” under the tax project. **Calls** contains the dentist. **Home** contains the parking fine. **Waiting** contains Priya under the tap project.

## Tuesday

You have 20 minutes on the bus. You open **Anywhere** and email the accountant. You tick that subtask. The tax project has no live action, and you know the next one, so you add “Gather the interest statements” tagged `anywhere` immediately.

You do not open **Home**. You are on a bus.

## Wednesday

Priya sends the photos while you are chatting; **When Messaging** surfaces the waiting item, and you tick it and add “Book the plumber from the shortlist” with tag `anywhere` under **Kitchen tap fixed**.

**Today** also shows nothing dated, so you leave **Today** alone.

## Thursday morning

You are at home. **Home** shows “Pay the parking fine”. You pay it and tick it. You flag “Gather the interest statements” because you want it done before the weekend, then do it from **Flagged** while the kettle boils. You tick it and add “Call the accountant with the questions list” tagged `call` on the tax project.

## Thursday, leaving the house

**Out** is empty. That is fine. You had no errands. You do not invent an errand to make the list feel used.

## Friday review

You open **Weekly Review**.

- **Inbox** has two new items (“buy a washer for the tap”, “look at spare room paint”). The washer becomes an `out` subtask on the tap project, because you will pick it up in town. The paint stays in **Someday** next to the spare-room idea.
- **Waiting** is empty.
- Both projects have a current tagged subtask.
- **Next Actions** still has the dentist call. It is still one step.
- **Someday** still holds bread and the spare room. Both stay.
- You look at your horizons note, see “sort the tax year without a last-minute panic”, and leave the tax project as it is because it already serves that.
- You clear the flag, which is already done.

Then you open **Calls** and book the dentist, because the review reminded you the action existed and you have the phone in your hand.

## What this week did not need

No `@computer` tag. The email and the bank payment were **Anywhere** and **Home**, based on the real constraint. No tag per person: Priya was a waiting title plus When Messaging. No energy tags. No separate later list: “pay anything owed” waited in the project notes until the return was further along.
