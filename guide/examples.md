This is one ordinary week for someone restarting after a quiet couple of months. The point is the shape of the decisions, so you can copy the moves with your own words.

## Monday afternoon: the dump

They speak Inbox full on the walk home:

- tax return
- tap still dripping
- call dentist
- maybe learn to bake bread
- Priya owes the damp photos
- parking fine on the kitchen table
- email accountant the questions
- idea: repaint the spare room

## Monday evening: clarify

| Inbox line | Decision | Where it ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tax return | More than one step, and they are committed | Project in Money: **Tax return filed**. Notes hold “pay anything owed” for later. Subtask: “Email accountant the questions” `#anywhere`. The separate “email accountant” inbox line is the same action, so it is deleted. |
| tap still dripping | A project, because someone has to look at it and then it has to be fixed | Project in Home: **Kitchen tap fixed**. Subtask: “Priya: photos of the damp patch” `#waiting`, When Messaging set to Priya. They realise the photos are about the tap, so “Priya owes the damp photos” is that same subtask. |
| call dentist | One phone call | Next: “Call dentist and book a checkup” `#call`. |
| maybe learn to bake bread | Not a commitment | Someday, untagged. |
| parking fine on the kitchen table | One step, needs the house because the letter is there | Next: “Pay the parking fine” `#home`. |
| idea: repaint the spare room | Not a commitment this month | Someday, untagged. |

Inbox is empty. Anywhere contains “Email accountant the questions” under the tax project. Calls contains the dentist. Home contains the parking fine. Waiting contains Priya under the tap project.

## Tuesday

They have twenty minutes on the bus. They open **Anywhere** and email the accountant. They tick that subtask. The tax project has no live action, and they know the next one, so they add “Gather the interest statements” `#anywhere` immediately.

They do not open Home. They are on a bus.

## Wednesday

Priya sends the photos while they are chatting. When Messaging surfaces the waiting item. They tick it and add “Book the plumber from the shortlist” `#anywhere` under **Kitchen tap fixed**.

Today also shows nothing dated, so they leave Today alone.

## Thursday morning

They are at home. **Home** shows “Pay the parking fine”. They pay it and tick it. They flag “Gather the interest statements” because they want it done before the weekend, then do it from **Flagged** while the kettle boils. They tick it and add “Call the accountant with the questions list” `#call` on the tax project.

## Thursday, leaving the house

**Out** is empty. That is fine. They had no errands. They do not invent an errand to make the list feel used.

## Friday review

They open Weekly Review.

- Inbox has two new items (“buy a washer for the tap”, “look at spare room paint”). The washer becomes an `out` subtask on the tap project, because they will pick it up in town. The paint stays in Someday next to the spare-room idea.
- Waiting is empty.
- Both projects have a current tagged subtask.
- Next still has the dentist call. It is still one step.
- Someday still holds bread and the spare room. Both stay.
- They look at their horizons note, see “sort the tax year without a last-minute panic”, and leave the tax project as it is because it already serves that.
- They clear the flag, which is already done.

Then they open **Calls** and book the dentist, because the review reminded them the action existed and they have the phone in their hand.

## What this week did not need

No `@computer` tag. The email and the bank payment were Anywhere and Home, based on the real constraint. No tag per person: Priya was a waiting title plus When Messaging. No energy tags. No separate later list: “pay anything owed” waited in the project notes until the return was further along.
